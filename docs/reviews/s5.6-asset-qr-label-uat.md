- Physical QR payload is exactly the canonical LARAS public URL `https://laras.bakaranproject.com/q/{publicId}`; the random UUID is the only record identifier in that URL.

- A logged-in browser does not silently expand the anonymous response.
- Sensitive/richer information requires LARAS login, an active same-School membership, and the normal RBAC permission.
- QR scan/login grants no new permission.
- QR rotate/revoke preserves history and uses QR token-version If-Match.
- Label batches/items are immutable snapshots; reprint is append-only evidence.
- Reprint revalidates current Asset + active QR snapshot and fails closed when stale.
- Label rendering must not mutate Asset, Device, Inventory, custody, Incident, Work Order, TESSELA, or Operational Calendar authority.
- Global Laboratory Context limits candidate discovery but is not a security authority.
